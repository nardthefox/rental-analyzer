Yearly amortization summary

Handles fixed rates, interest-only periods, an ARM reset at the assumed reset rate, and mortgage insurance drop-off. A balloon month is flagged; the balance then continues as if refinanced on the same terms.

30-year projection and IRR

Year 1 is the first year of stabilized operation. Fixed expenses grow at the expense-growth rate; percent-of-rent expenses follow rent. IRR assumes a sale at the end of that year, net of selling costs. Prepayment penalties are not modeled.
